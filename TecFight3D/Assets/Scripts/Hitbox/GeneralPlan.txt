a hurtbox is formed out of 11 colliders for humanoids. 
Variable to fit the shape of the character. 
1 hurtbox generator per character
They are bound to the rig.
A script that takes a list of (bound bone, hitbox size, hitbox shape)
Tagged as hurtbox.
hurtboxes will be active every frame from the beginning of the game
hurtboxes are passive, they just exist for detection

A hitbox is formed out of N colliders.
These colliders spawn and despawn at certain points in the move
Hitboxes have priority and indexes
Hitboxes can be bound to a bone or standalone
Hitboxes have send Angles attached to them
Hitboxes will be spawned by the animations
Hitboxes will be tagged as such
Hitboxes are active, and will execute the code for damage, knockback, and hitstun

Hitbox active benefits, less code executing every frame
hurtbox active benefits, less interscript communication
